# Cutisie Cute art brief

Cutisie Cute is the mascot of L2RR2L: her real-life digital pet unicorn. Reference pictures are in `refs/`.

**Look to match exactly:** a tiny, fluffy, glowing white baby unicorn; huge sparkly blue eyes; soft pink inner ears; a white spiral horn; a wild fluffy white mane; translucent pearly-pink bat/dragon-style wings; a thin tail ending in a fluffy tuft; soft rim-light glow, Pixar-like 3D render. No people in any image. Background: deep navy night sky with soft blue star bokeh, unless the item says otherwise.
